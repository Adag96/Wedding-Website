Daphne and Adam fell in love in the forest. Well, they met in Forest Park. They fell in love in Kew Gardens and grew in love between shoreline Connecticut and Sunnyside gardens. You are invited because you are a part of their love story.

Itinerary

Our magical evening will kick off at 5:30pm. There are two stone cottages around the perimeter of Hessian Lake. Prepare for an autumn breeze before we warm up by the fire!

Dress fabulously. If you think you’re “too much” we encourage you to be more. If you wear a Venetian mask you will be made an honorary Italian (like Daphne). We would like you to come as yourself, otherwise!

At 6pm, we’ll gather on the ground level of Bear Mountain Inn for cocktails, snacks and acoustic tunes. Greet the newlyweds in their final boss forms (Bride and Groom).

We’ll head upstairs at 7pm for dinner, cake, dancing and sacred time together! If you’re a late-night party person, we have plans to continue the fun just around the corner from the reception hall until 1:30am. Please plan responsibly if you intend to drive in!

This region of Bear Mountain State Park offers a variety of fun activities ranging from a vintage merry-go-round to a trailside zoo featuring rescued & rehabilitated wild animals and local geology.
